Put your own N.O.V.A. 3: Near Orbit Vanguard Alliance v1.0.7 APK plus its two
OBB expansion files in this directory. The filenames do not matter.

N.O.V.A. 3 PortMaster port and eapx extractor by EapRules.

The port validates this exact native library before extracting anything:

  lib/armeabi-v7a/libNOVA3_neon.so
  SHA1 ae97a4423fc6b9d003cc534fd4a4d03e6d0df0c0

(the APK also carries libNOVA3_8_neon.so, an older build; the port ignores it.)

A complete donor is three files:

  the APK           (game code, ~34 MB)
  main.1050.com.gameloft.android.ANMP.GloftN3HM.obb    (~1.85 GB)
  patch.1070.com.gameloft.android.ANMP.GloftN3HM.obb   (~325 MB)

The supported build is v1.0.7, Android package
com.gameloft.android.ANMP.GloftN3HM. Any of these three shapes works - drop
whichever one your copy came as, next to the APK:

  the two .obb files loose in this directory
  an Android/obb backup zip, unpacked or still zipped
  that backup's extracted com.gameloft.android.ANMP.GloftN3HM folder

The first launch extracts and validates everything automatically, then starts
the game. No game data is ever distributed with this port.
